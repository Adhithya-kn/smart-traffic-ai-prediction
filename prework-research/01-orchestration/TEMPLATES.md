# Standard Artifact Templates (T1 – T6)

## Template T1: Requirements Register Entry
```markdown
### FR-[ID]: [Title]
- **Category:** [Ingestion | Feature | Modeling | Simulation | Dispatch | Visualization]
- **Source:** [Dataset `[D]` | Literature `[L]` | Sample Notebook `[S]`]
- **Description:** [Precise requirement statement]
- **Validation Metric:** [How it is verified]
- **Priority:** [P0 (Core) | P1 (Essential) | P2 (Advanced)]
```

## Template T2: Literature Paper Analysis Card
```markdown
### Paper: [Filename]
- **Title:** [Full paper title]
- **Publication Venue / Year:** [Journal/Conference, Year]
- **Core Methodology:** [GNN, LSTM, XGBoost, etc.]
- **Key Findings:** [Quantitative results reported]
- **Direct Relevance to Our System:** [How our design utilizes this paper]
```

## Template T3: Architecture Decision Record (ADR)
```markdown
# ADR-[ID]: [Decision Title]
- **Status:** [Proposed | Accepted | Superseded]
- **Decider:** [Agent or Committee]
- **Context:** [Problem statement and forces]
- **Decision:** [Selected option and rationale]
- **Consequences:** [Positive, negative, and trade-offs]
```
